Types of Joins:
 
INNER: Fetch matching record only from both table. 

select employee.empid,employee.empname,employee.city,employee.deptid,department.deptname,department.deptsize from employee INNER JOIN department ON employee.deptID = department.deptID;
+-------+---------+------+--------+------------+----------+
| empid | empname | city | deptid | deptname   | deptsize |
+-------+---------+------+--------+------------+----------+
|     1 | omkar   | pune |      2 | F_devloper |       10 |
|     2 | karan   | pune |      3 | B_devloper |        8 |
|     3 | kartik  | pune |      1 | HR         |        5 |
+-------+---------+------+--------+------------+----------+

LEFT: fetch all record from left table.

select employee.empid,employee.empname,employee.city,employee.deptid,department.deptname,department.deptsize from employee LEFT JOIN department ON employee.deptID = department.deptID;
+-------+---------+------+--------+------------+----------+
| empid | empname | city | deptid | deptname   | deptsize |
+-------+---------+------+--------+------------+----------+
|     1 | omkar   | pune |      2 | F_devloper |       10 |
|     2 | karan   | pune |      3 | B_devloper |        8 |
|     3 | kartik  | pune |      1 | HR         |        5 |
+-------+---------+------+--------+------------+----------+

RIGHT : fetch all record from right table.

select employee.empid,employee.empname,employee.city,department.deptid,department.deptname,department.deptsize from employee RIGHT JOIN department ON employee.deptID = department.deptID;
+-------+---------+------+--------+------------+----------+
| empid | empname | city | deptid | deptname   | deptsize |
+-------+---------+------+--------+------------+----------+
|     3 | kartik  | pune |      1 | HR         |        5 |
|     1 | omkar   | pune |      2 | F_devloper |       10 |
|     2 | karan   | pune |      3 | B_devloper |        8 |
+-------+---------+------+--------+------------+----------+

FULL OUTER : fetch all possibility from both table.
             By using UNION keyword.

select employee.empid,employee.empname,employee.city,employee.deptid,department.deptname,department.deptsize from employee LEFT JOIN department ON employee.deptID = department.deptID UNION select employee.empid,employee.empname,employee.city,department.deptid,department.deptname,department.deptsize from employee RIGHT JOIN department ON employee.deptID = department.deptID;

CROSS : It will build cross connection betn two table & fetching all possibility.

select employee.empname,department.deptname from employee CROSS JOIN department;

SELF : A self join is a join where a table is joined with itself.
       It is useful records in the same table are related to each other.
